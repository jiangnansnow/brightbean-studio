Data Deletion / 数据删除

How to delete your account and data / 如何删除您的账号和数据

Self-Service Deletion / 自助删除

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time directly from the app settings. This is the fastest and most complete method.

您可随时在应用设置中直接删除您的账号及所有相关数据。这是最快、最彻底的删除方式。

1
Log In / 登录
Sign in to your Brightbean Studio account at v.ysec.cn
在 v.ysec.cn 登录您的 Brightbean Studio 账号
2
Settings / 设置
Go to your profile settings page
进入个人资料设置页面
3
Delete Account / 删除账号
Find the "Delete Account" option and confirm
找到"删除账号"选项并确认
4
Done / 完成
Your account and all data will be permanently removed
您的账号及所有数据将被永久删除

What Gets Deleted / 删除范围

  • Your account profile and registration information.您的账号资料及注册信息。
  • All content you created or uploaded (posts, images, videos, drafts).您创建或上传的所有内容（帖子、图片、视频、草稿）。
  • Connected social account tokens and authorization records.已连接的社交账号令牌及授权记录。
  • Analytics and activity logs associated with your account.与您的账号相关的分析及活动日志。

Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Effective date / 生效日期: 2026-10-01

1. Who We Are / 我们是谁

This policy applies to v.ysec.cn, a self-hosted social media management service (BrightBean Studio) operated by the site owner ("we/us"). Contact: zytzenger@163.com

本政策适用于 v.ysec.cn——由站点所有者（我们）运营的自托管社交媒体管理服务（BrightBean Studio）。联系方式：zytzenger@163.com

2. Information We Collect / 我们收集的信息

Account registration data (name, email address); content you create or upload (posts, images, videos); social account connection tokens (e.g. Facebook, Instagram via Meta Platforms) that you explicitly authorize; technical logs (IP address, browser, access times).

账号注册信息（姓名、电子邮箱）；您创建或上传的内容（帖子、图片、视频）；您明确授权的社交账号连接令牌（如通过 Meta 平台的 Facebook、Instagram）；技术日志（IP 地址、浏览器、访问时间）。

3. How We Use Information / 信息使用方式

We use the information solely to provide the service: scheduling and publishing content to your connected social accounts via official first-party APIs (including the Meta Graph API), synchronizing comments and messages into a unified inbox, and generating analytics reports.

我们仅为提供服务而使用信息：通过官方第一方 API（包括 Meta Graph API）向您连接的社交账号定时发布内容、将评论和私信同步到统一收件箱、生成数据分析报告。

4. Data Sharing / 数据共享

We transmit data to Meta Platforms (Facebook, Instagram, Threads) only as required to execute the API actions you request. Data is hosted on our own server. We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

我们仅在执行您请求的 API 操作所必需时向 Meta 平台（Facebook、Instagram、Threads）传输数据。数据存储于我们自己的服务器。我们不会出于营销目的向第三方出售、出租或共享个人数据。

5. Data Retention and Deletion / 数据保留与删除

Data is retained while your account is active. You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data at any time by emailing zytzenger@163.com; deletion requests are completed within 30 days. You can also disconnect social accounts at any time, which revokes our access tokens.

账号存续期间数据将被保留。您可随时发送邮件至 zytzenger@163.com 请求访问、更正或删除您的数据；删除请求将在 30 天内完成。您也可随时断开社交账号连接，这将撤销我们的访问令牌。

6. Cookies / Cookie 使用

We use only session cookies required for login and security. No advertising or third-party tracking cookies are used.

我们仅使用登录和安全所必需的会话 Cookie，不使用广告或第三方跟踪 Cookie。

7. Security / 安全措施

All traffic is encrypted via HTTPS. Social account tokens and credentials are stored encrypted at rest. Access to administrative functions is restricted.

全站通过 HTTPS 加密传输。社交账号令牌与凭证在存储时加密。后台管理功能访问受限。

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

本政策的任何变更将发布在本页面并更新生效日期。
